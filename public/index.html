MY PERSPECTIVE

From patterns
to understanding.

My work brings together data analysis and physical interpretation to explore how ozone and circulation evolve together.

Through discussions with my supervisor and collaborators, I work to connect those patterns to a clearer understanding of Arctic extremes and their predictability.

03ABOUT ME

Following the atmosphere,
across scales and places.

I am a doctoral researcher at the Institute of Geosciences (IGEO–CSIC–UCM) and Universidad Complutense de Madrid. My background spans atmospheric science, climate change, and climate data analysis.

Before my PhD, I explored the possible links between Greenland snowmelt and North Atlantic climate at the Niels Bohr Institute.
